Notes from the shoreline

The Sun Drenched Journal

The joy of a slower beach day

Pack only what you need, leave room for a friend, and let the day unfold. Sometimes the best adventure is a familiar stretch of sand, a long conversation, and no hurry to head home.

Small ways to show the ocean some love

Bring a reusable water bottle, keep an eye on anything that could blow away, and pick up a little extra litter before you leave. Small habits feel even better when you share them.

After the swim

A little care goes a long way.

Always follow the care label for your piece. After a salty or sandy day, rinse swimwear in cool fresh water, gently press out excess water, and let it dry in the shade. Avoid wringing, rough surfaces, and leaving damp pieces bundled in your beach bag.

Keep jewelry dry between wears and store it gently. For questions about a specific piece, we’re a message away.
